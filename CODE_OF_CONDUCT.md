# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive project community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation, never on people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, or share another person's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to Aaditya Singh through the repository's
maintainer contact channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled fairly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation
when this code is violated.
